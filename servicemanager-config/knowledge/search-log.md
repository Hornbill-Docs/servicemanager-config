# Search Log

The Knowledge search log helps analyze what people are searching for. This can help provide insight into how to organize your knowledge and identify gaps.

Search data is collected from

* The Employee portal [search widget](/servicemanager-config/employee-portal/search-widget).
* The Employee portal [knowledge widget](/servicemanager-config/employee-portal/knowledge-widget).
* The Knowledge search on the [Knowledge Browse](/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/search-and-browse) view.

## Before you begin

* To access the **Search Log** in Configuration, the [Knowledge Administrator role](/servicemanager-config/setup/service-manager-roles#knowledge-roles) is required.

## How to access

1. Select the Configuration cog icon located at the bottom of the left hand vertical menu.
2. Select `Service Manager`.
3. Locate the section titled `Knowledge Base`.
4. Select `Search Log`.

## Search Log Data

![Search Data](/_books/servicemanager-config/knowledge/images/search-log.png)

* **Date.** The date/time that the search was run.
* **Search Term.** The full search that was entered by the user.
* **Results.** The number of articles that were returned in the search results.
* **Source.** Where the search was initiated from.
* **Searched By.** The user who performed the search.
* **User Type.** Identifies whether the person performing the search is a basic user, full user, or contact.

A valuable aspect of search data is identifying instances of someone having searched for something without getting any results. Getting no results --- or in some cases, too many results --- can impact the usefulness of your Knowledge content. Using this data can help you continually improve your Knowledge content. Ask yourself:

* Are there any articles covering what they are looking for?
* Do existing articles contain the correct text?
* Where are most of the searches coming from?
* Is a search term trending on a particular day?

Use the filter to show searches that contain a particular word.

* How many times has this word been included in a search?
